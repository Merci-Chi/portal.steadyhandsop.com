Steady Hands LLC

Website-Specific Services Agreement

Version: October 14, 2026 · Agreement version 2026-10-14-project-v1

One agreement for each website.

This agreement applies solely to the website project named in the Client Portal immediately before electronic signature. A second website requires its own signed agreement, development payment and hosting subscription.

Development: $100 one-time per website, unless an expressly approved website-specific promotion applies.
Hosting: Standard $20/month or Business $30/month per website, depending on the plan selected at checkout. Any applicable taxes or additional charges must be disclosed before payment.

1. Parties, Project and Scope

This Agreement is between Steady Hands LLC (“Steady Hands,” “we”) and the individual or organization accepting through the Client Portal (“Client,” “you”). It covers only the identified website project (“Website”). The project brief submitted in the portal describes your requested pages, design preferences, supplied materials and requested functions. Features that are not expressly approved are not promised. Changes to scope or price require Client approval before additional charges.

2. Development Fee and Timeline

A $100 one-time website-development fee applies to each Website unless an authorized promotion states otherwise. Payment is due before development begins. A quote for work outside the agreed scope must be approved separately. Any estimated timeline depends on Client providing requested materials, approvals and access promptly; no launch date is guaranteed unless confirmed in writing.

3. Monthly Hosting and Automatic Renewal

Hosting is purchased separately for each Website: Standard Hosting is $20/month; Business Hosting is $30/month. Each Website has its own recurring subscription. Hosting automatically renews monthly until canceled. The applicable price, billing start date, billing interval and any tax will be presented for approval at checkout. By completing the subscription checkout, Client separately authorizes the stated recurring charges. A signed Agreement by itself does not authorize a charge.

4. Hosting Plans, Updates and Extras

Standard Hosting includes website hosting, maintenance and content-update requests through the portal. Business Hosting includes Standard Hosting and supported admin-dashboard capabilities, subject to the Website's technical features. Neither plan includes unlimited redesigns, third-party subscriptions, custom integrations or new features unless specifically agreed. We may quote additional services separately and will not charge for unapproved extras.

5. Cancellation and Service End

Client may request cancellation in the Client Portal or by emailing the support contact shown there. Cancellation of one Website does not cancel any other Website. Unless stated otherwise at checkout, cancellation stops future renewals and service continues until the end of the paid billing period. We do not refund elapsed or partially used billing periods except where required by law or where an express refund promise applies. After service ends, the hosted Website may stop being publicly accessible. Client should request an export before the paid term ends; available export formats depend on the technology and third-party licenses involved.

6. Development Refunds and Acceptance

The one-time development fee becomes nonrefundable after substantive work begins, except where required by applicable law or where we fail to provide an agreed remedy. If work has not begun, Client may request cancellation and a refund of the development fee. Client will have a reasonable opportunity to review a preview and report material issues against the approved scope. We will work in good faith to correct verified issues within that scope.

7. Ownership, Domains and Content

Client retains ownership of original content, logos, trademarks and materials Client supplies. Client is responsible for having rights to those materials. Ownership of a custom Website deliverable, source code and design assets depends on what is expressly identified as transferable in the project scope and on payment in full; third-party templates, software, fonts, plugins, subscriptions and Steady Hands preexisting tools remain subject to their respective licenses and ownership. Client-owned domain names remain Client property; Steady Hands will provide reasonable cooperation for transfer of a domain under Client ownership, subject to registrar requirements and amounts due. This Agreement does not promise transfer of proprietary hosting infrastructure or licensed third-party software.

8. Client Responsibilities and Content

Client must provide accurate information and timely decisions, maintain account security, and obtain required permissions for content and images. Client is responsible for the legality of information published at Client's direction, including required privacy notices, terms, accessibility obligations and industry-specific disclosures. Steady Hands is responsible for its own work and will not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

9. Security, Availability and Third Parties

We will use reasonable care to operate hosting and maintain access controls, but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability or security. Internet networks and outside services may interrupt availability. Client should retain independent copies of essential materials. Each party will promptly notify the other upon learning of a material security issue involving the Website. Separate privacy terms may apply to personal information processed through the portal or Website.

10. Changes, Suspension and Disputes

We may suspend service for material nonpayment, unlawful use or immediate security threats, with notice when reasonably practicable. We will provide notice of material hosting-price changes before they take effect, and Client may cancel before the new price applies. The parties agree to attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith written notice first. Neither party waives any nonwaivable consumer protection, statutory remedy or legal right.

11. Liability

Neither party is responsible for indirect or consequential losses to the extent the law permits. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, infringement or any other liability that cannot lawfully be restricted. Any additional limitation of liability must be separately agreed in writing and consistent with applicable law.

12. Electronic Signature and Records

Client may review and retain this Agreement before signing. By checking the acknowledgment and drawing an electronic signature, Client intends to sign this Agreement for the specific Website identified in the portal. Steady Hands should retain a durable copy of the signed version, project identity, signature and timestamp and make a copy available to Client. Electronic signatures are governed by applicable law. Checkout provides separate payment and recurring-billing consent.

13. Entire Agreement and Contact

This Agreement, the identified Website's approved scope and any separately accepted checkout terms form the agreement for that Website. In the event of a conflict over price or recurring billing, the terms explicitly accepted at checkout govern those charges, subject to law. Updates to this Agreement do not retroactively change a signed Website agreement without Client consent. Notices and cancellation requests may be sent using the contact information provided in the Client Portal.
